Quick answers

How long does a project take?

A typical business website takes three to six weeks. Online shops, apps and custom systems take longer, and you get a timeline in the plan before you commit.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need. After a short call we send a written quote that lists exactly what is included. Marketing is billed monthly.

Do I have to use both software and marketing?

No. Many clients start with one. Using both means the website and the campaigns are planned together, which saves time and money.

Can you build a full online store?

Yes. From a simple catalogue to a full e-commerce platform with product management, secure checkout, M-Pesa and other online payments, stock tracking, delivery options and an admin dashboard. If your customers prefer to order on WhatsApp, we can connect that too.
