Direct answers

How long does a project take?

Business sites: three to six weeks. Commerce platforms, apps and custom systems: scoped per build, with a fixed timeline in the written plan.

What does it cost?

Priced per scope. After a short call, a written quote itemises every deliverable. Marketing runs on a monthly retainer.

Are software and marketing sold together?

No. Each runs independently. Combined engagements share one plan, one data layer and one point of accountability.

Can you build a full e-commerce platform?

Yes. Product management, secure checkout, M-Pesa Daraja STK Push with callback reconciliation, stock tracking, delivery rules and an admin dashboard on Cloudflare D1 or Supabase. Branch-specific WhatsApp order routing is available.
