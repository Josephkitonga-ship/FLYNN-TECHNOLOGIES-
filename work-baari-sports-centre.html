Baari Sports Centre

A sports shop online, with categories, sale prices, a cart and a live chat button.

Client
Baari Sports Centre
Live website
baarisportscentre.co.ke
What we did
Web design and development
Visit the live site

The challenge

Baari Sports Centre sells football boots, jerseys and accessories. Its customers needed to scan a lot of products quickly on a phone, spot the deals and buy without friction.

What we built

  • A bold dark design with a lime accent that matches the energy of sport
  • Category tabs for All, Boots, Jerseys, Accessories and Training, so shoppers jump straight to what they want
  • Product cards with sale badges, the current price in KES, the old price crossed out, and a wishlist heart
  • Add to Cart on every card, a cart counter in the header and a search button
  • A floating WhatsApp chat button that follows the customer down the page

What customers can now do

  • Find boots, jerseys or training gear in a tap
  • Spot sale prices and the saving instantly
  • Save favourites and add items to the cart
  • Ask the shop a question without leaving the page
