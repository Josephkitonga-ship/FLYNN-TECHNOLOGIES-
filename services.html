Questions we hear often

How long does a website take?

A typical business website takes three to six weeks from the first call to launch. Apps and custom systems take longer, and we give you a timeline in the plan before you commit.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope. After a short call we send a written quote with what is included, so there are no surprises. Marketing is billed monthly.

Is AI right for my business?

Sometimes. We start by looking at which tasks repeat every day, such as answering the same questions or copying orders, and tell you honestly whether automation will save you time.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We build sites so you can change text, photos and prices without a developer, and we show you how during handover.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain, content and website belong to you. We hand over the accounts and files when the project is complete.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We can redesign it, add features such as online payments, booking or stock management, or take over its updates and marketing.

Do you work outside my town?

Yes. We work across Kenya, and most of the work happens by WhatsApp, phone and video call.

What happens after launch?

We stay available. You can add a support plan for hosting, updates and fixes, or hand over everything and manage it yourself.
