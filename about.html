Why Flynn exists

Joseph Gitonga founded Flynn Technologies to give businesses one dependable team for the technology and the marketing.

Too many businesses end up with a good website nobody visits, or good marketing that sends people to a site that does not work. Flynn builds the two together so each makes the other better.

We build for the way customers really shop in Kenya: on a phone, on mobile data, and and with mobile payments such as M-Pesa. That shapes every page, every form and every button we make.

Our mission

Build technology that works, and use it to help our clients grow.

Our approach

Plain language, written plans and regular updates. You always know what is happening and why.

How we work

  • Ship things that work

    We test on real phones and real connections before anything goes live.

  • Be honest about time and cost

    You get a written scope and price first. If something changes, we tell you early.

  • Measure what matters

    We track the results that affect your business, not numbers that only look good.

  • Stay after launch

    A launch is the start. We keep improving, fixing and promoting.

Working with us

What to expect from the first message to launch day and beyond.

  1. One point of contact

    You talk to the same person throughout, mostly on WhatsApp, with calls when they help.

  2. Progress you can see

    You review the work at each stage and approve it before we move on.
