Privacy policy

How Flynn Technologies handles your information. Last updated October 2026.

Who we are

Flynn Technologies is a software engineering and digital marketing company in Kenya, led by Joseph Gitonga. You can reach us at josephflynn774@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Details you send us through the contact form, email or WhatsApp: your name, company, email address, phone number, project details and budget range.
  • Basic visit information such as pages viewed and device type, if analytics is switched on for this site.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We use trusted services to run this site, such as website hosting, a form service that delivers contact messages to us, and WhatsApp when you choose to message us. These providers process information only to provide their service.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to work with you, then delete them when they are no longer required.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email us and we will respond. You may also contact the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
